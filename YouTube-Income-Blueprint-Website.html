Before you begin.

A clear picture of what you're getting, so you can decide if it fits your next step.

Is this suitable if I've never made a video?+

Yes. The guide starts with the basics, including what a channel is, how to create one and how to choose a format. Each chapter gives you practical steps and a task to finish, so you can build as you learn.

Do I need paid tools or expensive equipment?+

The manual workflows begin with a free-first approach. Start with your existing phone or computer and simple software that meets the task. If you choose paid software or hire freelancers, those costs are separate from the e-book.

Does it cover both Shorts and long-form videos?+

Yes. The book includes separate Shorts and long-form paths and helps you choose one primary format. You can focus on the format that suits your audience, idea and available time.

What exactly will I receive?+

One downloadable 184-page PDF with 24 chapters, 32 copy-ready prompts, worksheets, checklists and a 30-day action plan. You can read it on a computer, tablet or phone. The worksheets are inside the PDF; separate editable templates, video lessons, coaching and freelancer services are not included.

Is the guide relevant outside the US?+

Yes. This English-language edition is written for a worldwide audience and uses USD for its illustrative financial examples. Available tools, platform features and monetization eligibility can vary by country; check the current official requirements for your location.

Will it guarantee views, monetization or income?+

No. It gives you a practical process for creating, publishing and improving your videos, plus guidance on how creator monetization works. Results depend on your content, execution, audience and platform requirements. The 30-day plan is an action schedule, not an income promise.
